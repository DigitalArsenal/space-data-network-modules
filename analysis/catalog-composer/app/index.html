    Object matching

    Check a proposed crosswalk against two trajectories. Use canonical OEM FlatBuffers sampled by a propagator on the same inertial UTC grid. Each file must contain one object, one uniform block, and at least five position/velocity samples.

    ⓘ How matching works

    Provider IDs and datefirst associations propose candidates. The WASM module compares the whole supplied arc and independently differentiates positions to check velocities. Compatible means agreement within your thresholds, not proven identity or a probability. A single encounter cannot establish identity. No catalog record is merged by this check.

    Vimpel native elements must first be converted and propagated with verified frame, epoch and angle conventions. These uploads accept normalized OEM products, not raw Vimpel files. Threshold defaults are review settings and require calibration for your data.
