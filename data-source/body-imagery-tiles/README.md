# body-imagery-tiles

A body's high-definition global imagery as the console's tile pyramid, in the files the engine's `imageryFromTileFiles` reads for the Moon (`data-source/moon-imagery`). First source: Mars, the USGS Astrogeology THEMIS Day IR global mosaic 100 m v12 (NASA/JPL/ASU, public domain), planetocentric on the 3396190 m MOLA sphere. Level 9 (81.4 m/px at the equator) is the deepest.

Served from R2 bucket `spaceaware` through https://terrain.spacedatanetwork.org (CORS on); no node, no IPFS.

```
imagery/499/root.dtts                    levels 0-2 (42 tiles)
imagery/499/<L>/<x/16>_<row from south>.dtts   16 tiles of one row, levels 3-9
imagery/499/MANIFEST.json                credit, licence, source edition, files/bytes per level, ledger digest
```

Each file is a size-prefixed `$DTT` stream (`[u32 LE length][record]`); one record per 256 px baseline JPEG tile (`GEOGRAPHIC_TWO_ROOT_BODY`, `PAYLOAD_FORMAT CUSTOM`, `image/jpeg`, quality 88, grayscale, 0 = no data).

Credit: THEMIS: NASA/JPL/ASU (Christensen et al. 2004; Edwards et al. 2011,
JGR 116, E10008); mosaic USGS Astrogeology. Public domain.

## Module (pure transforms, WASM, single thread)

- `tiles_plan`: level-9 tile row -> the whole-row byte range of the mosaic (`If-Match` on its ETag) and the unit.
- `tiles_direct`: unit + the range's `HttpResponse` -> the row's 1024 tiles in 64 files (area average of the measured source pixels).
- `tiles_reduce`: level L row from the two child rows' files (JPEG decode, 2 x 2 mean of the measured pixels, JPEG); level 2 builds `root.dtts` from level 3.

## Tools

- `tools/encode.mjs --out DIR --level 9 --workers 4` (then `--level 8..3 --children DIR`, `--level 2`): resumable, byte-identical at any worker count, `DIR/ledger.jsonl` of path/bytes/sha256/md5.
- `tools/publish.mjs`: `aws s3 sync` of a level and its verification against the bucket listing (size and ETag = ledger md5), then `--manifest`. `R2_AWS` names the command that runs `aws` against the bucket.
- `tests/themis.e2e.test.mjs`: the module against GDAL's own read of the source (SSIM, 20 IAU features, layout, pyramid); `THEMIS_VERIFY_R2=1` adds the bucket and live-HEAD checks.

Build: `node build.mjs`.

## Small bodies and giant planets (0.3.0)

The unit's `naif` (499 Mars THEMIS when absent) selects a source of `src/sources_small.hpp`, generated by
`tools/small/gen-sources.py` from `tools/small/bodies.json` and each file's own headers: Phobos 401, Io 501, Europa 502, Ganymede 503,
Callisto 504, Mimas 601, Enceladus 602, Tethys 603, Dione 604, Rhea 605, Titan 606, Iapetus 608, Triton 801, Pluto 999, Charon 901 (USGS
Astrogeology global mosaics; Phobos is ESA CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO) and Jupiter 599 (Cassini PIA07782), Saturn 699, Uranus 799, Neptune 899
(HST OPAL, with its acknowledgement). Gray or RGB (planes one after another, one range each: `rows_0` .. `rows_2`); a body of one or two
levels (the giants) is one unit and writes root.dtts alone.

- **Longitude.** Every USGS product read in this table states its longitude PositiveWest in its label, and none is mirrored: the
  GeoTIFF geotransform read literally as east longitude puts the IAU gazetteer's craters on the encoded tiles (tools/small/verify.py,
  crater contrast peaks at no mirror and 180 E, z 4 to 21). The OPAL maps are 360 W at the left edge decreasing to the right, which is
  east longitude increasing from 0.
- **Derived sources.** Mimas (USGS COG, LZW tiles) is read as the same pixels in uncompressed strips (`gdal_translate -co COMPRESS=NONE
  -co TILED=NO -co BLOCKYSIZE=1`). Titan is the 450 m Float32 partial (65 S to 45 N) over the 4 km global, one 8-bit raster on the
  450 m lattice (`tools/small/titan-composite.py`: quantile-matched to the 4 km values, a smoothed low-frequency correction, 32 px feather).
  The giants' planetographic latitude is converted to planetocentric (`tools/small/giant-derive.py`). `etag` is `derived:<file>` and the
  encode reads those ranges from `--derived`.
- **Run.** `node tools/small/encode.mjs --body <key> --out DIR [--derived DIR] --workers 2` (deepest level from the source, coarser
  levels reduced from it, root.dtts last; byte-identical at any worker count), `R2_AWS=... node tools/small/publish.mjs --body <key> --out DIR`,
  test `SMALL_WORK=<dir> node --test tests/small.e2e.test.mjs`.

## Large bodies (sources_big.hpp, tools/big/)

Mercury (199, MESSENGER MDIS BDR 166 m, level 8), Venus (299, Magellan SAR left-look 75 m, level 10), Ceres (2000001, Dawn FC LAMO 35 m, level 7) and Vesta (2000004, Dawn FC LAMO 20 m, level 7), chosen by the unit's NAIF id. Each source states its file's own frame (`lon0_deg`, x0/y0 from the GeoTIFF or PDS label), planetocentric east longitude taken literally; `tests/big.e2e.test.mjs` registers 10 IAU gazetteer features per body within one pixel (Ceres: Kait system, Vesta: Claudia Double Prime, the frame of the console's pck00011). Venus stops at 80 S and 84 N: its no data (and holes) is filled bilinearly from the C3-MDIR 2025 m mosaic (`fill_0` range; C3-MDIR itself ends at 82.4 S, the south pole stays no data). A unit makes up to 256 files (level 10 is 128 a row).

`tools/big/run.mjs --source KEY --out DIR [--chunk N]` encodes, uploads, verifies and reduces a whole pyramid with bounded local disk (`R2_AWS` runs aws against the bucket); `encode.mjs` / `publish.mjs` are its steps.
